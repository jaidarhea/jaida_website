Polar Science Research Laboratory · Clark University

Distributed Biological Observatory

My research in Clark University's Polar Science Research Laboratory under the mentorship of Dr. Karen Frey is focused on the Distributed Biological Observatory (DBO), which is a change detection array in the Pacific Arctic region. The DBO was created to track the broad biological responsse to sea ice retreat and associated environmental change in the region, and includes selected biological measurements at multiple trophic levels alongside hydrographic measurements and satellite observations. Specifically, my role includes comparing NASA's PACE-OCI (Plankton, Aerosol, Cloud, and ocean Ecosystem-Ocean Color Instrument) ocean color data with NASA's Aqua-MODIS (Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer) ocean color data.
