Hello, welcome.

My name is Jaida Rhea and I am a graduate student completing my Master's degree at Clark University. My work lives at the intersection of climate dynamics and earth systems science, bridging geographic analysis frameworks and complex environmental data arrays to track shifting landscape behaviors.

Prior to my graduate studies, I earned a B.A. in Environmental Science alongside a second major in Professional and Creative Writing from Goucher College in Baltimore. This dual background shapes my daily approach to research—allowing me to combine heavy computational mapping and remote sensing workflows with precise, accessible storytelling.

My broader work involves tracking physical environmental markers, field data arrays, and exploring regional vulnerabilities across rapidly changing landscapes.
