Biography

My name is Jaida Rhea and I am a graduate student at Clark University in Worcester, Massachusetts where I am pursuing my M.S. in Climate and Society. I am particularly interested in coastal Arctic environments, and my current research interests encompass using near-surface remote sensing to advance understading of Arctic vegetation responses to climate change, permafrost dynamics, and the connection between coastal and marine environments. In addition, I hold a B.A. in environmental science and professional and creative writing from Goucher College in Baltimore, Maryland.

My past research and internship experiences have ranged from studying high-Arctic tundra vegetation using the PhenoCam Network with the U.S. Naval Research Laboratory and wetland conservation work with the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) to working on environmental justice projects with the Maryland Institute for Applied Environmental Health and the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center. While my ultimate goal is to be a climate scientist, I am passionate about science communication and blieve writing and developing effective and accessible communication strategies have a very important role to play in bridging the gap between public understanding and scientific consensus.
